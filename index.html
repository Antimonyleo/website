Nucleic acid nanotechnology

Hao Liu, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Fellow · Arizona State University

I design interaction rules that make nucleic-acid components react, fold, and assemble into prescribed structures. Combining computational modeling with experiments and structural characterization, I revise those rules when competing outcomes appear.
