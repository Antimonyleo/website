One scaffold, three open crystal lattices

A tetrahedral DNA-origami building block can select among cubic diamond, hexagonal diamond, and ice 0 lattices through changes made only to its four sticky ends.

Open tetrahedral lattices share the same local fourfold coordination, which makes selecting one long-range structure over another a difficult assembly problem. This preprint holds the nanoscale scaffold fixed and changes only the programmed interactions at its vertices.

The result is selective assembly of three distinct open crystal topologies from the same tetrahedron-shaped DNA origami. The work shows how competition-aware interaction design can control structure without requiring a new scaffold for every target.
