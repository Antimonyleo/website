---
title: "Building finite objects and lattices from DNA polycubes"
date: "2025-12-24"
summary: "An inverse-design platform programs DNA-origami cubes to form finite three-dimensional objects and extended lattices while screening out competing assemblies."
publicationId: "polycubes-2025"
---

DNA polycubes treat DNA-origami cubes as programmable building blocks for larger three-dimensional structures. The inverse-design workflow finds a small, target-specific set of cube types and interactions for a chosen object.

The same framework supports finite objects and extended lattices while explicitly designing against unwanted alternatives. It provides a route from a target geometry to an experimentally realizable assembly rule set.
